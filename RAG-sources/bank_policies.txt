FIRST NATIONAL BANK - ACCOUNT POLICIES

OVERDRAFT PROTECTION:
- Automatic transfer from savings to checking
- $5 transfer fee per occurrence
- Must maintain $50 minimum in savings
- Available 24/7 for eligible accounts

WIRE TRANSFER LIMITS:
- Domestic: $25,000 per day
- International: $15,000 per day
- Additional verification required for amounts over $10,000
- Processing time: Same day for domestic, 1-3 days international

MOBILE DEPOSIT LIMITS:
- Personal accounts: $5,000 per day
- Business accounts: $10,000 per day
- Checks over $1,000 may have extended hold periods
- Image quality requirements apply

ACCOUNT CLOSURE PROCEDURES:
- 30-day written notice preferred
- All outstanding transactions must clear
- Automatic payments should be redirected
- Final statement issued within 10 business days

CREDIT CARD REFUND POLICY:
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days of merchant approval
- Refund credits appear on next billing statement
- For purchases made in foreign currency, refund amount may vary due to exchange rate fluctuations
- Disputed transactions: temporary credit issued within 10 business days pending investigation
- Annual fee refunds: prorated if card cancelled within first 60 days
- Cash advance fees are non-refundable
- Interest charges on refunded purchases will be credited in the following billing cycle
- Refunds exceeding current balance will result in a credit balance on the account
- Credit balance can be requested as a check or left to offset future charges
- Refund processing may take longer during peak holiday periods (up to 14 business days)
- Send claims to ccrefund@bankone.com
